About V.M.P.S

A UK company, built for Libya's oil sector

Vortex Multi Petroleum Services Ltd (V.M.P.S) is a UK-based company operating across Libya, dedicated to supporting the oil and gas industry with reliable, high-quality service.

We work directly with oil companies throughout the country, supplying the parts and tools their operations depend on, and standing behind every delivery with responsive after-sales support and ongoing maintenance.

Our model pairs UK and European sourcing standards and quality control with a genuine local presence — teams based in Libya who understand the pace and demands of field operations. That combination lets us respond quickly, communicate clearly, and keep our clients' equipment running with minimal disruption.

In Libya, our operations are carried out through our sister company, Al-Moobda Al-Masi Company for Importing Industrial and Marine Equipment, which manages local registration, import, and on-the-ground activity on our behalf.

What We Do

Services

Four core services, delivered as one continuous line of support — from first supply to ongoing maintenance.

SVC-01

Parts Supply

Sourcing and supplying industrial parts and components for oil and gas operations, matched to each client's equipment and specifications.

SVC-02

Tools Supply

Providing the operational and maintenance tools field teams need for day-to-day work at oil facilities and job sites.

SVC-03

After-Sales Support

Ongoing support once parts and tools are in the field — consultation, troubleshooting, and replacement when it's needed.

SVC-04

Maintenance

Scheduled and emergency maintenance for equipment and machinery, aimed at reducing downtime and keeping operations on track.
